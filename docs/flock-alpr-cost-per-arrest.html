From late 2023 to mid-2026, Caldwell Police made roughly 4,100 arrests. The cameras were part of 80 of them, about 2½ a month. CPD averages about 128 arrests a month.

Council members have said that dropping the cameras would mean hiring many more officers. CPD averages about 21 arrests per officer per year. The cameras were part of about 30 a year, roughly what one and a half officers already do.

How these numbers were figured. Dollar amounts are full contract totals, the way the city budgets them, including years not yet billed. The 80 arrests come from CPD's own slide to council, which gives no date range. We assume the count runs from installation (about Nov 2023, per the 2023 council memo) to the July 2026 presentation, about 32 months. CPD's total arrests come from Idaho State Police reports: 1,637 in 2024 and 1,444 in 2025. Late 2023 and 2026 are estimated at those years' rates. Even if all 80 ALPR arrests happened in 2024–25 alone, they would be 2.6% of arrests. The officer count (73) is from the city's police recruiting page, which is undated.
